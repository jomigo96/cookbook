Rice cooker banana bread/cake
-----------------------------

:term:`Easy`, :term:`Vegetarian`

Source: :cite:cts:`andycooks:banana_bread`

.. image:: /graphics/banana_cake.jpg
   :width: 40%
   :align: center

Ingredients
^^^^^^^^^^^

* 4 ripe :term:`Bananas <Banana>`
* 230g :term:`Flour`, the original recipe uses 120 plain and 110 wholemeal
* 50g :term:`Oats`, plus some more for sprinkling
* 60ml :term:`Milk`
* 120ml/108g :term:`Olive oil`
* 2 :term:`Eggs <Egg>`
* A spoon of baking powder
* Some ground :term:`Cinnamon`
* Optional: 100g dark :term:`Chocolate`. If omitted, you may want to add sugar.

Steps
^^^^^

On a large bowl, mash the bananas with a fork.
Add the eggs, and scramble.

Mix all the dry ingredients except the chocolate, plus a pinch of salt, and add it to the bowl.
Mix through.

Finally, crack the chocolate into small chips and add it to the batter.

Grease the rice cooker bowl and move the batter there.
Sprinkle some rolled oats on top.

Cook it in one regular rice cycle, around 40 minutes.
