Okonomiyaki
-----------

:term:`Easy`

.. image:: /graphics/okonomiyaki.jpg
   :width: 40%
   :align: center

Ingredients
^^^^^^^^^^^

For two servings:

* 100g :term:`Flour`
* 300g :term:`Cabbage`, a crunchy kind
* :term:`Green onion`
* Two :term:`eggs <Egg>`
* 150ml :term:`dashi` stock, warm
* A tablespoon :term:`baking powder`

Optional to add to the pancake:

* Pickled :term:`ginger`, diced
* :term:`Mushroom`, diced
* :term:`Shrimp`, diced
* :term:`Pork` belly, either pre-fried or sliced very thinly on top

Toppings:

* :term:`Nori` shreds
* :term:`Katsuobushi`, do not skip
* :term:`Mayo`
* Okonomiyaki sauce

Steps
^^^^^

Chop the cabbage thinly, and slice some green onions.

Mix the flour, baking powder and some salt on a bowl, then add two eggs and scramble.
Slowly add the dashi while mixing, be careful to not cook the eggs.
Add the cabbage, green onions, and everything else you put in the pancakes, and incorporate.

Warm-up a non-stick frying pan, brush some oil, then add the batter, up to 1 cm thick.
Fry on medium for around 5 minutes on each side, it should be golden brown.

.. note::

   For flipping, use a plate, and make sure there is still enough oil in the pan.
   It's also a good idea to keep jiggling it so it browns evenly.

Move it onto a plate.
Brush a lot of okonomiyaki sauce on top, then mayo (not brushed), then everything else.
