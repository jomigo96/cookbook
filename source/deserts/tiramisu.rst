Tiramisu
--------

:term:`Medium`, :term:`Vegetarian`

Adapted from: :cite:cts:`dessertperson:tiramisu`

.. image:: /graphics/tiramisu.jpg
   :width: 40%
   :align: center

Ingredients
^^^^^^^^^^^

For a large tray, around 6 servings

* Ladyfinger cookies
* 500g :term:`mascarpone`
* 400ml heavy :term:`cream`
* 5 :term:`Egg` yolks
* 75g :term:`Sugar`. You can use more.
* :term:`Cocoa powder`
* 400ml :term:`coffee`, preferably strong, freshly brewed
* Marsala, brown rum, or some other liqueur, optional
* 1 teaspoon :term:`vanilla extract`, optional

Steps
^^^^^

Brew some pour-over dark roast coffee, using slightly higher coffee-water ratio than for normal drinking coffee.
Add a couple tablespoons of liqueur and let it cool down.

Add the egg yolk and sugar to a bowl, and beat it over a gentle double boil until it roughly doubles in size and has a stiff texture.
It takes 10 to 15 minutes, afterwards let it cool down.

On a separate bowl, beat the mascarpone, heavy cream, splash of liqueur and vanilla until it is almost like whipped cream.

Add the egg mixture to this bowl and incorporate very gently.
It's ok if it's not completely homogeneous.

Assembling
""""""""""

Get a rectangular deep tray.
Form the base out of the ladyfingers, which are dipped in coffee for just 2 seconds.
Do not over dip or it will be too soggy.

Top it with the cream.
You can do multiple layers here.

Dust the top with a generous amount of cocoa powder.

Let it rest overnight in the fridge.
