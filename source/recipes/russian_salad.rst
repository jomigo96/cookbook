Russian salad
-------------

*salda russa* - a Portuguese variant.
In some places it is known as Olivier salad.

:term:`Easy`, :term:`Vegetarian`-ish, :term:`Leftovers`

.. figure:: /graphics/russian_salad.jpg
   :width: 40%
   :align: center

   ..

   Here it is shown with very little mayonnaise in order to be healthier.
   Normally one would use much more.

Ingredients
^^^^^^^^^^^

The ubiquitous ingredients are

* :term:`Potato`
* :term:`Carrot`
* :term:`Egg`
* :term:`Mayo`

Other common ones are

* Canned :term:`tuna`
* :term:`Peas`
* :term:`Onion`
* :term:`Beans`, especially black eyed beans
* :term:`Olives`
* Pickles

Steps
^^^^^

Prepare hard-boiled eggs.

Dice all the vegetables and the eggs into small cubes.
Boil the carrots, potatoes, and any other hard vegetables for around 15 minutes until tender.
Wait until completely cooled and dry.

Combine all the ingredients with plenty of mayonnaise.
Adjust the salt, and optionally season with pepper, a splash of vinegar, and some onion greens.
Serve cold.
