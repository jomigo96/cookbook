Tofu bake
---------

:term:`Easy`, :term:`Vegetarian`, :term:`One pot`

.. image:: /graphics/tofu_bake.jpg
   :width: 40%
   :align: center


Ingredients
^^^^^^^^^^^

* Firm :term:`tofu`
* :term:`Potato`
* Any other firm vegetables, like :term:`cauliflower`, :term:`carrot`, :term:`asparagus`

Seasonings:

* 1-2 tablespoons of :term:`olive oil`
* 1 tablespoon of :term:`honey`
* Paprika, or smoked paprika, or both
* Garlic powder
* Chilli powder
* Cumin, only a tiny bit
* Pepper

Steps
^^^^^

Cut every vegetable into small cubes.
Tear apart the tofu with your hands, trying to make irregular pieces.

Add everything into a baking dish, and season with a generous amount of salt.
Then, add all the remaining seasonings, and mix everything well.

Bake at 200C for 30 minutes, flipping things midway.
Towards the end, you can increase the heat a bit to crisp up the top.
