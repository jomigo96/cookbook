Stuffed peppers
---------------

:term:`Medium`, :term:`One pot`

.. image:: /graphics/stuffed_peppers.jpg
   :width: 40%
   :align: center

Ingredients
^^^^^^^^^^^

* Large :term:`bell peppers <Bell pepper>`
* Ground :term:`chicken`, or any other meat
* :term:`Onion`
* :term:`Tomato`, and a bit of paste
* :term:`Chilli peppers <Chilli pepper>`, a good amount of any kind
* Any shredded :term:`cheese`

Seasonings
""""""""""

Use a Mexican spice blend, like for tacos.
Alternatively, use:

* Garlic and onion powder
* Black pepper
* Cumin
* Oregano
* Paprika, preferably smoked
* Chilli powder, if not already spicy enough

Steps
^^^^^

Fry the meat with some olive oil on medium heat for a couple of minutes.
Add the diced onion, diced chilli peppers, some salt, and stir fry for a few more minutes.
Add a bit of tomato paste, and get a bit of browning, then add diced tomato and a bit of water to deglaze.
Let it cook on low for 5 minutes.

Remove from the heat, add all dry seasonings, some cheese, and mix.

Slice the bell peppers in half, lengthwise, and remove the white part.
Alternatively, slice the top and bottom bits and lay them upright.
Brush some olive oil on the outside, fill them up with the meat mixture, then top with more cheese.

Bake at 200°C for around 15 minutes.
